01 · About

Research with an engineer’s instinct.

I care about whether a model survives contact with the physical world: limited data, tight compute budgets, noisy sensors, and changing environments.

I am a PhD student in Information Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. My work spans computer vision, digital twins, multimodal learning, reinforcement learning, and trustworthy edge intelligence.

Before UMBC, I completed an M.Eng. at Kumoh National Institute of Technology in South Korea and worked as a Python software engineer. That combination shapes how I research: frame a concrete operational problem, build the learning system, and measure what changes.

My recent projects cover UAV tiny-object detection, industrial anomaly analysis, smart-warehouse optimization, emotion recognition, and privacy-aware AI.
