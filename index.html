01 · About

Research profile

PhD student at UMBC with experience spanning academic research, applied machine learning, and production software development.

I am a PhD student in Information Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. My current research investigates federated-LLM reasoning under heterogeneous data and scientific digital twins for mosquito-movement analysis.

Previously, I was a graduate researcher at Georgia Southern University and Kumoh National Institute of Technology, where I worked on UAV perception, multimodal hazard detection, industrial anomaly detection, edge vision, and warehouse automation.

Before graduate research, I worked as a Python software engineer at Divine IT, contributing to enterprise ERP, HRM, and applicant-tracking systems.
