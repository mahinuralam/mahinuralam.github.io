About me

Seeking Summer 2027 research internships in computer vision, federated learning, LLM reasoning, and digital twins. My background fits Research Scientist, Applied Scientist, and ML Research Intern roles, and I am always open to research collaborations. Email me.

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County.

I have over three years of combined academic and industry experience. Previously, I worked as a Graduate Research Assistant at PIC Lab, Kumoh National Institute of Technology, South Korea, where I contributed to research in industrial AI, multimodal learning, and warehouse automation. My research has been published in Q1 journals, including IEEE Access, ICT Express, and the Journal of Information Security and Applications, and at conferences such as the IEEE ICRA Workshop, IEEE ICC, IEEE ICMIC, and IEEE ICTC.

I also worked as a Software Engineer at Divine IT Ltd., where I developed enterprise software solutions and improved system performance through backend optimization and scalable software design.

Distributed Financial LLM

Federated instruction-tuning of Qwen-2.5-7B on a finance corpus with Flower, LoRA via PEFT, and FedAvg across 50 IID client partitions, trained on 2×RTX 3090 GPUs.

FIQA/FPB/TFNS avg 61.53 · 5th on the Flower leaderboard

  • LoRA
  • PEFT
Code

Federated Artificial Image Detection

A federated multi-attention CNN that detects AI-generated imagery across IID and non-IID clients, with weighted FedAvg, percentile clipping, and divergence monitoring for stable convergence.

RealAIGI 10k images incl. Sora · 100 clients

  • PyTorch
  • FedAvg
  • Attention
Code

Multimodal Emotion Recognition

A transformer-fused recognizer that denoises and synchronizes speech and EEG streams (MFCC features, FastICA filtering, dynamic time warping), with CNN-BiLSTM and BiGRU encoders joined by cross-attention.

97% accuracy · ternary affect classification

  • EEG
  • Speech
  • Cross-attention
Code

RL-based IoMT Intrusion Detection

A lightweight, cost-sensitive intrusion detection system for IoMT using CNN–LSTM and contextual-bandit PPO, with top-K Random Forest feature selection to cut false negatives at the edge.

97.99% accuracy · 1.2 ms latency · <0.15 MB model

  • CNN–LSTM
  • PPO
  • Edge
Code

Gumi Industrial Energy Forecasting

Visualized real industrial-complex energy data, applied smoothing and scaling, and built a lightweight CNN-BiLSTM forecaster.

  • CNN-BiLSTM
  • Time series
